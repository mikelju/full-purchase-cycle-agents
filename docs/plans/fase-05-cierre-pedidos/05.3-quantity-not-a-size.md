# Deviation 05.3 - The ordered quantity is not a size

Status: approved
Phase: `spec.md` and `plan.md` in this folder (increment 24, C21)

## What happened
The one live record of `order_scenarios` (increment 24, 2026-10-09, addf5ab) reached 86.7% [70.3, 94.7] (26 of 30) with 0 critical errors, under the 90% target, so it exited 3 and stored no baseline.
All four failures (OS-002 and OS-010 on WhatsApp, OS-012 and OS-020 on email) end with no order and a pending clarification although the model extracted the right SKU.

Cause, in the code and not in the model or the scenario expectation: `clarification.line_doubts` calls `candidate_search` on `line_text`, which for email and WhatsApp is the line `source_text`.
That text holds the ordered quantity, and `candidate_search` counts every figure of the text as a size figure.
A sibling whose size equals the quantity then ties with the intended product and raises an ambiguous doubt:
- "5 boxes of paper tape 2.5 cm" gives TAPE-PAP-25 and TAPE-PAP-50 (OS-002, OS-012).
- "10 units of sharps container 5 litres" gives SHARPS-10 and SHARPS-5 (OS-020).
- "2 units of sharps container 5 litres" gives SHARPS-2 and SHARPS-5 (OS-010).

Without the quantity each text gives one product.
The web form, whose product text holds no quantity, scored 100%.

## Options
| Option | Effect on criteria | Cost | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A (chosen): fix the doubt rule with regression tests, re-score every suite in replay, then re-measure `order_scenarios` | None; the 90% target and the gates stay | No new dependency; a re-record only if replay misses recordings, within the 1.04 USD left of the D9 ceiling | A re-scored figure could drop; the stop rule below applies |
| B: accept 86.7% as the result of C21 | C21 not met | None | The bug stays in the product |

## Fix scope
- For a free-text line (email and WhatsApp), `candidate_search` leaves out one figure token equal to the line's ordered quantity before it scores the figures.
- Only that one token is left out, not every equal number, so "8 boxes of sterile latex surgical gloves size 8" still picks size 8.
- Web form lines are unchanged, since their product text holds no quantity.
- A text with no other figure still gives every sibling, so a genuinely ambiguous line still raises a doubt.
- Known limit: when the quantity figure appears once and is really the size (for example "surgical gloves size 8" read as quantity 8), that token is left out and the line becomes ambiguous; asking is the safe side of that case.

## What does not change
- The 90% target of C21, its gates and the critical-error gate at 0.
- The 30 scenario files `evals/datasets/order_scenarios/OS-001.json` to `OS-030.json` stay as committed in bb46328.
- The recordings of addf5ab stay as recorded; a re-record happens only if the replay misses recordings, and it is a coordinator decision within the D9 ceiling.

## Re-scoring rules
- Every suite in `eval all` is replayed on its test split after the fix, tracing off, and compared with its stored baseline.
- Any drop of a re-scored figure against its baseline stops execution for an owner decision; no baseline or threshold changes in that case.
- `order_scenarios` is then replayed; its baseline is stored and it joins `eval all` only at 90% or more with 0 critical errors.

## Decision
The owner chose option A on 2026-10-10 via Lavish (`.lavish/fase-05-escenarios.html`).
